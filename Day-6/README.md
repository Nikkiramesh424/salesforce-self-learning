# Sprint 6 – Apex Triggers & Enterprise Trigger Architecture

## Overview

Sprint 6 focused on understanding how Salesforce applications respond automatically to business events using **Apex Triggers**. The sprint introduced the concepts of event-driven software, trigger architecture, trigger timing, and the importance of designing maintainable and scalable enterprise applications.

Rather than focusing on syntax, this sprint emphasized engineering principles, clean architecture, and separating business logic from trigger logic using Service Classes.

---

## Topics Covered

### Part I – Making Software Respond Automatically
- Importance of automation in enterprise applications.
- Understanding business events.
- Event-driven software architecture.
- Introduction to Apex Triggers.
- Why automation improves business efficiency.

### Part II – Understanding How Triggers Think
- Responsibilities of Triggers and Service Classes.
- Trigger architecture and clean design principles.
- Before vs After Trigger events.
- Business timing and event timing.
- One business event triggering multiple business processes.

### Part III – Building Enterprise Triggers That Stay Clean
- Enterprise Trigger architecture.
- Delegating business logic to Service Classes.
- Maintaining reusable and scalable applications.
- Trigger design best practices.
- Engineering principles for clean and maintainable automation.

---

## Key Learning Outcomes

- Understood the concept of event-driven software.
- Learned how Apex Triggers respond to business events automatically.
- Differentiated Trigger responsibilities from Service responsibilities.
- Understood Before and After Trigger execution.
- Learned to design clean, reusable, and scalable Trigger architecture.
- Appreciated the importance of maintainable enterprise application design.
