# Day 3 – Salesforce Interview Readiness Bootcamp

## Overview

The third day of the Salesforce Interview Readiness Bootcamp focused on Salesforce declarative automation and interview preparation. The assignments emphasized selecting the appropriate automation tool for different business scenarios while implementing practical solutions using Record-Triggered Flows and Validation Rules. The practical work was completed using a **Hospital OPD Management System**, where automation was applied to improve business processes and data quality.

## Tasks Completed

### 1. Interview Warm-up
Reviewed fundamental Salesforce concepts, including Validation Rules, Record-Triggered Flows, Apex Triggers, and the Salesforce Order of Execution to strengthen interview readiness.

### 2. Business Scenario Analysis
Analyzed real-world business requirements and identified the most suitable Salesforce automation tools based on functionality, maintainability, and best practices.

### 3. Design Challenge
Compared Validation Rules, Flows, and Apex Triggers for different business requirements and selected the most appropriate solution for each scenario.

### 4. Record-Triggered Flows
Implemented Record-Triggered Flows to automatically populate the Appointment Date and send email notifications when new Appointment records were created.

### 5. Validation Rules
Created Validation Rules to improve data quality by preventing invalid Patient Age, Appointment Date, and Consultation Fee values from being saved.

### 6. Trigger vs Flow Analysis
Compared the capabilities of Salesforce Flows and Apex Triggers and understood the situations in which each automation tool should be used.

### 7. Mini Project Enhancement
Enhanced the Hospital OPD Management System by implementing a Record-Triggered Flow that automatically creates a Prescription record whenever an Appointment Status changes to **Completed**.

### 8. Debugging Challenge
Analyzed automation conflicts between Flows, Triggers, and Workflow Rules and explored approaches to avoid recursion and improve system performance.

### 9. Interview Preparation
Practiced commonly asked Salesforce interview questions related to automation, Flow Builder, Validation Rules, Apex, and Salesforce best practices.

## Learning Outcomes

- Gained practical experience with Salesforce declarative automation.
- Built and tested Record-Triggered Flows for real-world business scenarios.
- Implemented Validation Rules to maintain data integrity.
- Improved understanding of Salesforce automation design and debugging.
- Strengthened interview preparation by answering scenario-based Salesforce questions.
- Enhanced the Hospital OPD Management System using Salesforce best practices.
